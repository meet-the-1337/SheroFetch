Shero brings back the song you name

He runs to my PC or phone and returns with lyrics.

    Shero’s machines report in here once the page loads.

    He fetches from my desk or my pocket

    Whichever machine is awake takes the next song. If both are asleep, Shero waits by the door.

    Pick a crayon, get a file

    Every song comes back tagged, with cover art and synced lyrics whenever they exist.

    Format

    ArtistAlbum

    • Artist - Title.mp3about 8 MB for a 4-minute song
    • Artist - Title.lrcsynced lyrics
    • cover.jpgalbum art

    Plays everywhere. Encoded at the best variable bitrate from the source.

    Throw the bone while he works

    Drag the bone and let go. He always brings it back, eventually.

    Keep Shero on your home screen

    Install the web app from your browser, or get the Android app. Both need an invite code to fetch.

    Download the Android app

    On a phone, open your browser menu and choose Add to Home screen.
